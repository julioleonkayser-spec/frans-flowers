Meet Fran

The story behind the shop

Francine Miller

Fran opened her first flower stand in the Mission District in 1995 with a single goal: bring a little color into people's days. Thirty years later, she still hand-picks the flowers for every weekly special.

When she's not arranging bouquets, Fran volunteers with local shelters and mentors young entrepreneurs in the neighborhood she grew up in.
